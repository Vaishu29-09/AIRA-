# Compliance Explanation

## Privacy-First Design
The system follows privacy engineering principles inspired by global data protection standards.

## Key Compliance Concepts Implemented

### Data Minimization
Only non-sensitive data is retained for analysis.

### Purpose Limitation
Redacted datasets are safe for secondary use like:
- Research
- AI training
- Public transparency initiatives

### Risk Reduction
Automated scanning reduces accidental exposure caused by:
- Human oversight
- Manual errors
- Large-scale datasets

### Anonymization by Default
Sensitive attributes are removed before data leaves the system.

## Why This Matters
Organizations can safely share data while reducing:
- Legal exposure
- Privacy violations
- Trust risks
